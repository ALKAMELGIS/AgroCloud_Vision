One record per field, shared by the whole team

The boundary is the start. Everything else the team does on that field attaches to it.

Boundaries

Draw them, or bring the files you already have

Trace a polygon or drop a pivot circle on satellite imagery. Or import a survey: every polygon becomes a field, its name comes from the file, and any projection is converted to WGS84 before it is saved in PostGIS.

GeoJSON KML / KMZ Shapefile File Geodatabase GeoPackage
Weather

A forecast for the field, not the city

Seven days of temperature and rainfall at each field's own coordinates, from Open-Meteo.

Area

Measured on the ellipsoid

Area is calculated by PostGIS on the WGS84 ellipsoid, so the hectares match a survey, not a flat screen.

Monitoring

See which part of a pivot fell behind

Sentinel-2 NDVI for each field with clouds masked inside the field, the hectares in each growth class, a season curve, and a date comparison that marks the areas that dropped against the rest of the field.

Operations

Crops and the activity log

Seasons and growth stages per field, and every fertilizer, spray, irrigation or harvest with its product and rate, on a monthly calendar you can export.

Team

Each organization sees only its own farms

Farms and fields are separated per organization in the database, and roles decide who can add or change fields: owner, manager, GIS analyst, agronomist, field supervisor or viewer.
