Everything the field work needs, nothing it doesn't

Three things the system does well today — more are on the roadmap, and we say so inside the app instead of pretending they already ship.

Farm & field management

Draw a field's exact boundary straight on the map — polygon or circle — and the system stores it in PostGIS and calculates its area automatically.

Real weather, per field

A live 7-day forecast for each field's own coordinates, pulled from Open-Meteo — temperature range, rainfall, and wind, not a generic city forecast.

Multi-tenant & role-based

Every organization's farms and fields are isolated at the database level, with JWT authentication and roles — Owner, Manager, GIS Analyst — enforced on every request.

From empty map to tracked field in three steps

1

Register your organization

Create an account and add your first farm by name — coordinates are optional, you can set them later.

2

Draw the field boundary

Open the farm, tap "Add Field," and trace the boundary directly on the map canvas — freehand polygon or a circle.

3

Track it from day one

The field's area, boundary, and weather are now live in your dashboard — the same record every other tool in the system builds on.
