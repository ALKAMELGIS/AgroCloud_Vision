How it works

From a boundary to a decision in six steps

  1. 01

    Add your fields

    Draw a polygon or a pivot circle, select it on the map, or import a Shapefile, KML, GeoPackage or File Geodatabase.

  2. 02

    Get the imagery

    Every clear Sentinel-2 date for the field appears on the timeline, with its cloud cover inside the boundary.

  3. 03

    Read the trend

    Plot several indices over the season, or switch to a heatmap of every day.

  4. 04

    Check the risks

    See which fields dropped since the last clear image and which part of the field is behind.

  5. 05

    Take action

    Send a scout to a point, build a variable rate map, or log the irrigation, spray or fertilizer you applied.

  6. 06

    Share the result

    Print the field report, export the zones for the terminal, or give your team the same view with their own role.

Inside the workspace

The tools you work with every day

Crop health indices

Pick an index and the field repaints with its own colour scale. Contrast stretches the colours within each field, so small differences inside a pivot stand out.

  • NDVI, SAVI and EVI for vigour, NDRE and GNDVI for nitrogen
  • NDMI and NDWI for water, BSI and SI for bare and salty soil
  • Click a value class in the legend to show only those pixels

Time series and heatmap

Plot one or several indices for a field over any period, with the spread inside the field and weather on its own axis. Switch to a calendar heatmap, a 3D skyline or a 3D pivot ring.

  • Line, area, bars, points or change from the previous date
  • Each clear date is one point; days between images are estimated and marked
  • Download the values as CSV

Field risks

Every field is compared with its previous clear image. The ones that dropped come first, so the morning starts with the right field.

  • Change in index value, not just a colour
  • Cloud cover inside the field shown with every date
  • Swipe or split the map to compare two dates

Variable rate maps

Build zones from one image, from the clearest dates of several seasons, or from up to five weighted layers. Give each zone a rate and see the total product and the saving.

  • Two to seven zones, smoothed, with a minimum zone size
  • Exports for John Deere, Trimble and any ISO-XML terminal
  • Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML and CSV as well

Soil sampling

Place sampling points on a grid or by zone, each one set away from the field edge, and take them to the field as GPX, KML, CSV or Shapefile.

  • Grid cells sized in hectares
  • One to five points in every productivity zone

Field activity log

Tillage, planting, fertilising, irrigation, spraying, pruning and harvest, each with its date, product and rate, on a monthly calendar for the whole farm.

  • Filter by type of operation
  • Download each month as CSV

Scout tasks

Drop a pin where the map shows a problem, say what to check, who goes and by when. Close it with notes when it is done.

  • Priority, issue type and due date on every task
  • Open tasks show as pins on the map

Weather analytics

Forecast and history at each field's own coordinates. Lay temperature, rain or soil moisture over the index curve to see what drove a change.

  • Seven-day forecast per field
  • Past weather for any period you plot

Field reports

Two A4 pages built from the field's own data: index and zone maps over satellite imagery, zone areas that add up to the field, the season curve, weather and operations, and notes written by rule from what the numbers show.

  • Areas in hectares, acres and dunums
  • Print or save as PDF from the browser

Team and roles

Each organization sees only its own farms. Roles decide who can change boundaries, who logs operations and who only reads.

  • Owner, manager, GIS analyst, agronomist, field supervisor and viewer
  • Field history keeps every boundary change, with restore

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Which satellite images does it use?

Sentinel-2 Level-2A from the European Copernicus programme, at 10 m per pixel. You can sharpen the view to 5 m or 2.5 m with a super-resolution model; the result is a model's estimate, not a new measurement.

How often is there a new image?

The Sentinel-2 satellites pass every few days. Each date is listed with the cloud cover inside your field, and cloudy dates can be hidden.

What happens when it is cloudy?

Clouds and their shadows are masked pixel by pixel inside the field, so a partly cloudy date still gives values for the clear part.

Which files can I import and export?

Import GeoJSON, KML, KMZ, zipped Shapefile, File Geodatabase and GeoPackage, in any projection. Export zones as ISO-XML, Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML, GPX or CSV.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The whole workspace, the reports and this site switch to Arabic with the layout mirrored from right to left.

What does it cost?

It is free during early access. Plans by area will follow, and you will be told before anything changes.
